## Summary
Black-and-white Haiku-style cursors with an animated busy indicator.

## Description
21 original Xcursor shapes (24, 32, 48 and 64 px) plus the usual alias names: arrow, pointing hand, I-beam, resize arrows, move, grab, help, copy, link, forbidden, pencil and an animated spinner in Haiku yellow. Missing shapes fall back to Breeze cursors.

Part of the Haiku global theme: STORE_LINK_GLOBAL_THEME
